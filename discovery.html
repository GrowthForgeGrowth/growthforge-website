Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Executive Growth Audit, what happens after purchase, and how GrowthForge evaluates whether we're the right long-term growth partner for your business.

What is an Executive Growth Audit?

The Executive Growth Audit is a comprehensive strategic assessment of your business, marketing, AI visibility, SEO, GEO, paid media, website performance, conversion optimization, analytics, and growth opportunities. You'll receive executive recommendations, prioritized initiatives, and a customized 90-day growth roadmap.

Who is the Executive Growth Audit designed for?

It's designed for founders, CEOs, business owners, B2B SaaS companies, healthcare organizations, professional service firms, and growth-focused businesses seeking strategic clarity before making larger growth investments.

What happens after I purchase an Executive Growth Audit?

Once your purchase is confirmed, we'll begin reviewing your business and contact you within 24 hours to schedule your private Executive Strategy Session. During the session, we'll present your findings, recommendations, and your customized 90-day growth roadmap.

Can Executive Strategy Sessions be scheduled internationally?

Yes. GrowthForge works with businesses across multiple regions and time zones. If you have scheduling preferences, simply let us know during your application or contact us directly, and we'll do our best to accommodate your availability.

What if I'm not sure which GrowthForge partnership I need?

That's exactly why every engagement begins with an Executive Growth Audit. We evaluate your business objectives, current performance, AI readiness, customer acquisition strategy, and growth opportunities before recommending the partnership that best aligns with your goals.

What happens if we're a good fit?

We'll discuss the Growth Partner, Growth Accelerator, or Growth Engine engagement that best supports your objectives, explain expected outcomes, implementation priorities, investment, and answer any remaining questions before moving forward.

What if we're not the right fit?

We'll tell you honestly. You'll still leave with your Executive Growth Audit, strategic recommendations, and actionable roadmap. Our goal is to provide objective guidance, even if a long-term partnership isn't the right next step.
