03 / AUDIT FINDINGS

The conversion gap was connected to the broader growth system.

Conversion was the clearest constraint, but the audit identified several supporting opportunities across search, AI visibility, content authority, and infrastructure.

01
BUSINESS ANALYSIS

The value proposition was stronger than the conversion architecture around it.

Goal Station has an integrated platform and clear commercial value, but the audit identified no clearly defined conversion pathways for its different audiences, including facilities, academies, and clubs.

OPPORTUNITY Build audience-specific pathways from value proposition to booking.
02
SEO & AI

Discoverability was established. Structured visibility had room to expand.

Goal Station demonstrated solid crawlability and strong bot access. However, limited internal linking and the absence of Product and FAQ structured data reduced the depth of the site's search and AI-readable architecture.

OPPORTUNITY Strengthen internal architecture and structured data for search and AI systems.
03
PERFORMANCE

Performance was an asset — not the problem to solve.

Performance scored 90/100, giving Goal Station a strong technical user-experience foundation. The recommendation was to protect this advantage while introducing the functionality required to improve conversion.

STRATEGIC DIRECTION Preserve speed while adding commercial functionality.
04
CONVERSION

The biggest immediate opportunity was lead capture.

Conversion scored 42/100. The audit identified zero email or phone links and no booking integration, creating friction between qualified interest and an actual commercial conversation.

PRIMARY OPPORTUNITY Establish clear CTAs, direct contact pathways, and booking infrastructure.
05
CONTENT & SECURITY

Authority and infrastructure needed additional development.

Content Authority scored 58/100, reflecting limited topical depth and authority signals. Security scored 55/100, with additional hardening opportunities identified around HSTS, CSP, and WAF implementation.

OPPORTUNITY Build topical authority while strengthening enterprise security controls.
EXECUTIVE INTERPRETATION
“The highest-impact sequence was to fix the commercial pathway first, then scale discoverability and authority around it.”
04 / GROWTH ROADMAP

Fix the commercial pathway. Then scale the system.

The audit identified a clear sequence for turning the existing foundation into a more complete growth engine. Conversion infrastructure comes first, followed by discoverability, authority, and ongoing optimization.

01
PRIORITY ONE IMMEDIATE

Repair the conversion pathway.

Establish a clear primary conversion action and remove unnecessary friction between visitor intent and a qualified business conversation.

  • Implement clear booking infrastructure.
  • Establish direct email and contact pathways.
  • Strengthen primary calls to action across key pages.
  • Create clearer audience-specific conversion paths.
02
PRIORITY TWO NEXT

Strengthen technical and AI discoverability.

Build on the existing technical foundation by improving semantic clarity, structured data, internal linking, and machine-readable signals.

  • Expand structured data implementation.
  • Strengthen internal-link architecture.
  • Improve entity and topical relationships.
  • Develop stronger AI-search readiness signals.
03
PRIORITY THREE BUILD

Develop topical authority.

Expand the content system around the audiences, problems, services, and search themes most relevant to commercial demand.

  • Build deeper topic clusters.
  • Create audience-specific content pathways.
  • Strengthen supporting internal links.
  • Develop authority and trust assets.
04
PRIORITY FOUR HARDEN

Strengthen infrastructure and security.

Address the remaining infrastructure opportunities identified during the audit while preserving the strong performance foundation already in place.

  • Strengthen HSTS configuration.
  • Evaluate CSP implementation.
  • Assess WAF requirements.
  • Maintain existing performance standards.
RECOMMENDED SEQUENCE
Convert existing demand before increasing demand.

The highest-leverage path is to establish the commercial foundation first, then compound its value through SEO, AI discoverability, content authority, and infrastructure improvements.
