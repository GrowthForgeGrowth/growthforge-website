Privacy Policy

GrowthForge is committed to protecting the personal and business information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage information when you use our website, submit an Executive Growth Audit application, or engage with our services.

Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. Introduction

GrowthForge ("GrowthForge," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy explains how GrowthForge collects, uses, stores, protects, and otherwise processes personal information when you visit our website, submit a form, purchase an Executive Growth Audit, communicate with us, or use our services.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the GrowthForge website, including information submitted through contact forms, Executive Growth Audit applications, consultation requests, and other interactions with our website or services.

By using the GrowthForge website or providing information to us, you acknowledge that you have been informed about the processing described in this Privacy Policy. Where applicable, we will obtain consent or rely on another lawful basis for processing as permitted by applicable privacy law.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you interact with GrowthForge. Depending on the service or interaction, this may include your name, email address, company name, company website, industry, number of employees, revenue range, marketing budget, growth challenges, services of interest, expected start date, and other information you choose to provide.

When you submit an Executive Growth Audit application or provide information for an audit, the information may also include business, marketing, advertising, analytics, conversion, customer acquisition, revenue, website, and other growth-related information necessary to perform the requested assessment.

We may also collect information automatically when you interact with our website. This may include information about pages viewed, sessions, interactions, referring sources, browser type, device information, approximate location information, website events, and other technical or usage information collected through analytics or similar technologies.

3. Information You Provide Through the Executive Growth Audit

GrowthForge's Executive Growth Audit is designed to evaluate business growth opportunities and constraints. Information submitted for an audit may therefore be more commercially detailed than information submitted through a general contact form.

Depending on the engagement, information may include business objectives, marketing performance, acquisition information, website and conversion information, advertising performance, analytics information, revenue-related information, customer acquisition information, and other business information that you voluntarily provide for the purpose of the audit.

We use this information to understand the business context, evaluate growth opportunities, identify potential revenue leakage or conversion constraints, prepare the Executive Growth Audit, develop strategic recommendations, and prepare the associated 90-day roadmap.

You should only provide information that you are authorized to provide and that is reasonably necessary for the requested service.

4. How We Use Information

GrowthForge may use personal and business information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries and requests submitted through our website.
  • To process Executive Growth Audit applications and service purchases.
  • To communicate with you regarding requested services and engagements.
  • To perform Executive Growth Audits and prepare strategic recommendations.
  • To schedule Executive Strategy Sessions and other requested meetings.
  • To provide requested consulting, strategy, growth, analytics, or related services.
  • To analyze website, acquisition, conversion, and growth information provided for an engagement.
  • To improve our website, services, processes, and customer experience.
  • To monitor website performance and understand how visitors use our website.
  • To maintain the security, reliability, and integrity of our website and systems.
  • To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and business obligations.
  • To communicate relevant business or service information where permitted by applicable law.

Where applicable, GrowthForge processes personal information based on the circumstances of the processing and the lawful bases available under applicable privacy law. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent subject to applicable legal and contractual limitations. Withdrawal of consent does not necessarily affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before the withdrawal.

GrowthForge seeks to collect and process information that is relevant and reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.
