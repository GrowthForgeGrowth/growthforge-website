A

AI Agent

An AI agent is an AI system that uses instructions, context, and tools to carry out multi-step tasks toward a goal.

For marketers, an agent may research, route leads, update approved records, or support campaign workflows. Why it matters: agents can extend automation beyond a single response, but need clear permissions and guardrails. Related terms: chatbot, workflow automation, and guardrails.

AI Content Generation

AI content generation is the use of AI tools to create or assist with text, images, audio, video, code, or content briefs.

It can accelerate research, drafting, repurposing, and production workflows, but useful content still requires intent alignment, accuracy checks, and human judgment. Related terms: generative AI, content optimization, and human-in-the-loop. Explore GrowthForge's AI content strategy insights →

AI Governance

AI governance is the policies, roles, controls, and review processes that guide responsible AI use.

Marketing governance can set standards for data handling, approved tools, brand claims, disclosure, human review, and accountability. Related terms: data privacy, guardrails, model monitoring, and transparency.

AI Marketing

AI marketing is the use of artificial intelligence to improve marketing strategy, execution, analysis, personalization, and optimization.

Teams apply AI marketing to content planning, customer insights, campaign operations, lead prioritization, and measurement. Why it matters: it helps teams make better use of data and reduce manual work when implementation is tied to business goals. Related terms: machine learning, generative AI, and marketing automation. AI Search

AI search is search that uses AI to interpret queries, retrieve information, or generate answers for users.

AI search can include AI-generated summaries and answer interfaces alongside traditional search results. Related terms: AI SEO, GEO, semantic search, and answer engine optimization.

AI SEO

AI SEO is the practice of improving search visibility with traditional optimization and AI-assisted analysis, prioritization, and content workflows.

It combines technical SEO, keyword strategy, content systems, and Generative Engine Optimization to address traditional search engines and AI-generated answers. Why it matters: it connects AI-assisted execution with the fundamentals of helpful, discoverable content. Read GrowthForge's AI SEO guide for B2B SaaS →

Algorithms

Algorithms are defined sets of rules or calculations that a system uses to process information and produce an outcome.

Marketing platforms use algorithms to rank search results, select ads, recommend content, and identify patterns in performance data. Related terms: machine learning, predictive analytics, and recommendation engines.

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Answer engine optimization is the practice of making content easier for answer-oriented search experiences to retrieve, interpret, and cite.

AEO overlaps with GEO and AI SEO, emphasizing clear answers, structured information, entities, and source-worthy content. Related terms: structured data and entity SEO.

Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence is technology that performs tasks associated with human intelligence, such as recognizing patterns, understanding language, and making predictions.

AI is the broad discipline; machine learning, natural language processing, and generative AI are more specific approaches or capabilities.

Attribution

Attribution is the process of assigning credit for a conversion or revenue outcome to marketing touchpoints.

Attribution models inform budget decisions, although every model has limitations because buyers often interact with multiple channels. Related terms: attribution confidence, conversion rate, and customer journey analytics.

Attribution Confidence

Attribution confidence is the degree to which decision-makers trust performance data enough to invest and scale with conviction.

Without reliable measurement across channels, budgets can flow to the wrong acquisition sources and CAC may be misunderstood. Related terms: attribution, data enrichment, and customer journey analytics.

Audience Segmentation

Audience segmentation is the practice of dividing an audience into groups with shared characteristics, needs, or behaviors.

Segmentation can improve messaging, offers, channel strategy, and personalization when it is based on useful customer signals. Related terms: first-party data and predictive lead scoring.

Automation

Automation uses software and rules to complete repeatable tasks with limited manual intervention.

In marketing, automation can route leads, trigger lifecycle messages, update CRM records, or report on performance. It does not always require AI. Related terms: workflow automation, marketing automation, and AI agent.

B

Bias

Bias is a systematic distortion in data, model behavior, or decision-making that can produce unfair, inaccurate, or unrepresentative outcomes.

In marketing, bias can affect audience selection, lead scoring, personalization, and content outputs. Related terms: training data, AI governance, and human-in-the-loop.

BOFU / MOFU / TOFU (Funnel Stages)

BOFU, MOFU, and TOFU describe where a buyer is in a decision journey: bottom, middle, and top of funnel.

BOFU content serves buyers ready to act, MOFU content supports evaluation, and TOFU content builds awareness through education. Related terms: search intent and topic cluster.

C

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

CAC is the total cost to acquire one new customer, including relevant marketing and sales spend.

Teams use CAC alongside conversion, revenue, and retention measures to evaluate growth efficiency. Related terms: customer lifetime value, CRO, and attribution.

Campaign Automation

Campaign automation is the use of software, triggers, and rules to coordinate campaign activities with less manual effort.

Examples include sending nurture emails, assigning leads, updating audiences, and creating reporting alerts. Related terms: marketing automation, workflow automation, and personalization.

Chatbot

A chatbot is a conversational interface that responds to user questions or requests through text or voice.

A chatbot may follow fixed rules or use an LLM. Unlike an AI agent, a chatbot does not necessarily take actions across tools or complete multi-step workflows.

Conversational AI

Conversational AI is technology that enables systems to understand and respond to people through natural language.

Marketers use it in chatbots, virtual assistants, customer support, and lead qualification experiences. Related terms: chatbot, natural language processing, and LLMs.

Content Optimization

Content optimization is the process of improving content so it better serves users, aligns with search intent, and supports a defined business goal.

For AI-assisted content workflows, optimization includes checking accuracy, clarity, topical coverage, internal links, and useful structure. Related terms: search intent, semantic search, and topic clusters. Explore GrowthForge's semantic SEO approach →

Conversion Optimization

Conversion optimization is the systematic improvement of pages, journeys, and offers to increase meaningful user actions.

These actions can include demo bookings, form submissions, purchases, or qualified leads. Related terms: CRO, conversion rate, and customer journey analytics.

Conversion Rate

Conversion rate is the percentage of users who complete a desired action out of the total users who had an opportunity to do so.

AI can help marketers identify patterns and friction points, but conversion rate should be evaluated alongside lead quality and business outcomes. Related terms: CRO, attribution, and engagement rate.

Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

CRO is the systematic improvement of landing pages, user journeys, and conversion architecture to increase the percentage of visitors who take meaningful actions.

Why it matters: CRO helps teams improve conversion before increasing paid media spend. Related terms: conversion optimization, conversion rate, and customer journey analytics. Explore GrowthForge's CRO partnership options →

Customer Journey Analytics

Customer journey analytics examines how people move across touchpoints before and after conversion.

It helps teams identify friction, channel interactions, and opportunities to improve the buyer experience. Related terms: attribution, CRO, and personalization.

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

Customer lifetime value is the estimated revenue or value a customer contributes over the full relationship with a business.

Marketers use CLV to evaluate acquisition efficiency, retention, segmentation, and investment decisions. Related terms: CAC, predictive analytics, and personalization.

D

Data Enrichment

Data enrichment is the process of adding useful information to an existing customer, prospect, or account record.

Enriched data can improve segmentation, personalization, lead routing, and analysis, provided teams respect privacy requirements and data quality standards. Related terms: first-party data, data privacy, and audience segmentation.

Data Privacy

Data privacy is the responsible collection, use, storage, sharing, and protection of personal or sensitive data.

For AI marketing, privacy affects what data can be used in prompts, models, personalization, and analytics workflows. Related terms: first-party data, AI governance, and guardrails.

E

Engagement Rate

Engagement rate is a measure of how often people interact with content, campaigns, or experiences relative to a relevant audience or exposure total.

Its calculation varies by platform, so marketers should define the denominator and engagement actions consistently. Related terms: conversion rate, attribution, and predictive analytics.

Entity

An entity is a distinct, identifiable thing or concept, such as a company, person, product, place, or topic.

Search engines use entities and their relationships to understand content beyond individual keywords. Related terms: entity SEO, semantic search, and structured data.

Entity SEO

Entity SEO improves clarity around who a brand is, what it does, and how concepts on its site relate to each other semantically.

Consistent organization details, author information, structured data, and clear concept relationships can support search visibility. Related terms: entity, semantic search, and GEO. Learn about GrowthForge's AI SEO and entity strategy →

F

Fine-Tuning

Fine-tuning is the additional training of an existing AI model on a narrower dataset for a specific task or style.

It differs from writing better instructions: prompt engineering guides a model at use time, while fine-tuning changes the model through training. Related terms: training data and LLMs.

First-Party Data

First-party data is information a business collects directly from its own audiences, customers, products, or digital properties.

Examples include CRM records, website behavior, purchase history, and customer feedback collected with appropriate consent. Related terms: data privacy, data enrichment, and personalization.

G

Generative AI

Generative AI creates new content, such as text, images, audio, code, or summaries, from a prompt or other input.

Marketers use it to support research, drafting, repurposing, and workflow acceleration while maintaining review for accuracy and brand judgment. Why it matters: it can speed up production, but it does not replace subject-matter expertise or editorial review. Related terms: large language models, prompts, and hallucinations. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

GEO is the discipline of optimizing content and entity signals so AI-powered search engines can retrieve and cite a brand in generated answers.

GEO differs from traditional SEO because it focuses on AI retrieval and citation as well as rankings and click-through rates. Related terms: AI search, entity SEO, and AEO. Explore GrowthForge's GEO services →

Growth Consultancy

A growth consultancy is a strategic partner that aligns marketing activity, measurement, and business goals around growth outcomes.

It differs from a traditional marketing agency by connecting strategy across channels and executive priorities. Compare growth consultancy vs. marketing agency →

Growth System

A growth system is an integrated operating model that aligns strategy, acquisition, conversion, measurement, and iteration toward business outcomes.
