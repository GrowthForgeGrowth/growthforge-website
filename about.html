About GrowthForge

Building the next generation of AI-powered growth consulting.

GrowthForge helps ambitious businesses build predictable growth through AI search, acquisition strategy, conversion optimization, automation, and executive consulting. We combine senior-level strategic thinking with AI-powered execution to deliver measurable business outcomes.

Founded by Charlie Chan, GrowthForge is a boutique consultancy focused on long-term partnerships, measurable results, and modern growth systems built for the AI era.

Why GrowthForge Exists

GrowthForge was founded on the belief that sustainable business growth requires more than isolated marketing tactics—it requires a connected growth system. As AI transforms how businesses are discovered, evaluated, and chosen, companies need a strategy that aligns visibility, acquisition, conversion, and customer retention into a single, measurable framework.

We partner with founders and leadership teams to build that framework. By integrating AI Search Optimization (AI SEO & GEO), paid media, conversion rate optimization, analytics, marketing automation, and executive growth strategy, we help businesses identify growth opportunities, remove bottlenecks, and create scalable systems that drive measurable business outcomes.

Every recommendation is tied to business objectives rather than marketing activity. Our goal is to help organizations build a repeatable growth engine that adapts to changing customer behavior, evolving search technologies, and an increasingly AI-driven digital landscape—creating long-term competitive advantage instead of short-term marketing wins.

Frequently Asked Questions About GrowthForge

What is a growth consultancy and how is it different from a marketing agency?

A growth consultancy like GrowthForge takes a strategic partnership approach focused on building predictable growth systems. Unlike traditional marketing agencies that charge for isolated services, we become an extension of your team, aligned with your business metrics, and committed to your long-term success. We think like business partners, not vendors.

Why did Charlie Chan found GrowthForge?

Charlie founded GrowthForge after years of seeing the marketing agency model fail ambitious businesses. He believed there had to be a better way—one where businesses get direct senior-level strategic thinking, AI-powered execution, and genuine partnership rather than commodity services.

What is your mission and vision?

Our mission is to help ambitious businesses build predictable growth systems through strategy, AI, and data-driven execution. Our vision is a future where ambitious businesses have access to senior-level strategic thinking and AI-powered execution without the overhead of building a full internal team.
