Executive Framework

AI Search Readiness Checklist

Review these foundational requirements before investing heavily in AI SEO, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), or large-scale content production.

  • Clearly identify the founder or subject-matter expert across commercial pages.
  • Publish an About page explaining experience, methodology, and operating principles.
  • Maintain consistent positioning, service descriptions, and contact information throughout the website.
  • Create clear pathways from educational content to commercial pages and conversion points.
  • Support commercial pages with authoritative educational content and strategic internal links.
  • Demonstrate genuine expertise through original frameworks, research, benchmark reports, and case studies.
