SaaS Growth Metrics That Actually Matter: 15 KPIs to Track

A practical framework for tracking SaaS growth metrics across acquisition, conversion, product activation, recurring revenue, retention, expansion, and efficiency.

GrowthForge • Published September 20, 2026 • Updated September 20, 2026 • Growth Strategy

SaaS growth metrics tell you whether your company is creating durable recurring revenue—or simply buying short-term growth that churn will erase later. The most useful metrics for SaaS growth connect the full customer lifecycle: acquisition, activation, conversion, retention, expansion, and efficiency.

A general operating KPI may describe activity, such as total signups or website sessions. A SaaS growth metric helps explain whether that activity creates lasting customer value, recurring revenue, and efficient growth. This guide covers the SaaS growth KPIs founders, marketers, product teams, and revenue leaders can use to prioritize decisions rather than maintain an unranked dashboard.

What are SaaS growth metrics?

SaaS growth metrics are measurements that show how effectively a subscription software business acquires qualified customers, helps them reach value, generates recurring revenue, retains accounts, expands existing revenue, and does so efficiently.

They answer questions such as:

  • Are we acquiring customers who are likely to succeed with the product?
  • Are new users reaching a meaningful first-value moment quickly?
  • Is recurring revenue growing through new business, expansion, or both?
  • Are customers staying long enough to justify acquisition costs?
  • Are existing accounts expanding, contracting, or churning?
  • Is growth becoming more efficient over time?

Unlike a single top-line revenue number, SaaS business metrics show the relationships between the customer lifecycle stages. They help teams distinguish compounding growth from growth that requires constant replacement.

15 SaaS growth metrics: summary table

Use this summary as a starting point for tracking the most important SaaS KPIs. The right dashboard will vary by business model, sales motion, contract size, and stage, but these metrics provide a balanced view of growth quality.

Core SaaS growth metrics, what they measure, and why they matter
Metric What it measures Why it matters
MRR growth rate Change in monthly recurring revenue Shows the direction and pace of recurring revenue growth.
ARR growth Annualized recurring revenue growth Supports longer-term planning and reporting.
New MRR Recurring revenue from new customers Shows new-business contribution to growth.
Expansion MRR Additional recurring revenue from existing customers Reveals upsell, cross-sell, and account-growth performance.
Customer churn Percentage of customers lost Shows account retention health.
Revenue churn Percentage of recurring revenue lost Shows the financial impact of cancellations.
GRR Starting revenue retained before expansion Reveals underlying revenue durability.
NRR Revenue retained and expanded from existing customers Shows whether the installed base compounds.
CAC Cost to acquire a new customer Measures acquisition efficiency.
CAC payback Time required to recover acquisition cost Indicates how quickly growth investment returns.
LTV:CAC Estimated customer value relative to acquisition cost Helps assess the quality of unit economics.
Lead-to-customer conversion Percentage of leads that become customers Measures funnel quality and sales effectiveness.
Activation rate New users or accounts reaching a value event Connects demand generation to product value.
Feature adoption Use of priority product capabilities Can identify behaviors linked to retention and expansion.
