Pricing FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

Learn what happens after your purchase, how GrowthForge partnerships work, and what to expect throughout your engagement.

What happens after I purchase an Executive Growth Audit?

Once your Executive Growth Audit purchase is confirmed, we'll begin a comprehensive assessment of your business, market, competitors, marketing performance, AI SEO readiness, paid media, and conversion opportunities. Within 24 hours, we'll contact you to schedule your Executive Strategy Session, where we'll present your findings, strategic recommendations, and a customized 90-day growth roadmap. If there's a strong fit, we'll also discuss the GrowthForge partnership best aligned with your business goals.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my GrowthForge partnership?

Yes. Many businesses begin with Growth Partner and later upgrade to Growth Accelerator or Growth Engine as their business grows. Your partnership can evolve alongside your objectives and implementation requirements.

What level of support is included?

Growth Partner includes monthly strategy sessions and email support. Growth Accelerator includes bi-weekly strategy sessions with priority support. Growth Engine includes weekly executive meetings, strategic planning, and the highest level of implementation support.

What are the partnership terms?

Growth partnerships require a minimum three-month commitment. Most clients remain engaged for twelve months or longer to maximize long-term growth, operational improvements, and measurable business outcomes.

Can I add Growth Accelerators later?

Yes. Specialized Growth Accelerators—including Technical SEO Audits, AI Workflow Automation, CRM Automation, Executive Workshops, Programmatic SEO, Landing Page Design, Analytics Dashboards, and AI Chatbots—can be added to any partnership.

What if I'm not ready for a long-term partnership?

The Executive Growth Audit is designed for businesses that want strategic clarity before committing to ongoing consulting. You'll receive an executive assessment, prioritized recommendations, and a practical 90-day roadmap you can implement immediately.

How do you measure success?

Every partnership is measured against business outcomes. Depending on your objectives, we track qualified leads, conversion rates, customer acquisition costs, revenue growth, return on ad spend, customer lifetime value, AI Search visibility, and other executive KPIs.

What happens if we're not seeing results?

GrowthForge operates with complete transparency. If priorities need to change or a different strategy will produce better outcomes, we'll recommend those adjustments. Our objective is long-term business growth, not simply delivering predefined marketing activities.
