What is GrowthForge's AI visibility policy?

GrowthForge is optimized for both traditional search engines and AI-powered search experiences. We support responsible indexing while maintaining transparent policies governing how our content may be discovered, accessed, and referenced by AI systems.

Can search engines index the GrowthForge website?

Yes. GrowthForge allows indexing by major search engines, including Google and Bing, helping businesses discover our AI-powered growth consulting services through organic search.

Does GrowthForge allow AI training on its content?

Yes. GrowthForge currently declares ai-train: yes and permits approved AI crawlers through our robots.txt configuration to support responsible AI discovery and indexing.

Which AI crawlers are currently permitted?

GrowthForge currently allows major AI and search crawlers, including GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, Google-Extended, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Meta-ExternalAgent, Applebot, Applebot-Extended, Amazonbot, and other widely recognized search engine crawlers.

Can the AI visibility policy change in the future?

Yes. We periodically review and update our AI visibility, indexing, and crawler policies to reflect changes in AI search technology, industry best practices, and our long-term business objectives.
