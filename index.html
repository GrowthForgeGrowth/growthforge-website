Frequently Asked Questions About GrowthForge

What is a growth consultancy and how is it different from a marketing agency?

A growth consultancy like GrowthForge takes a strategic partnership approach focused on building predictable growth systems. Unlike traditional marketing agencies that charge for isolated services, we align strategy, execution, and optimization to your business outcomes over the long term.

How does GrowthForge use AI in growth strategy?

We use AI across multiple dimensions: AI SEO for search visibility optimization, AI-powered Google Ads and Meta Ads bidding and targeting, marketing automation to scale execution, predictive analytics for decision-making, and workflow automation to improve speed and quality.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and why does it matter?

Generative Engine Optimization is optimizing your content and website for AI search engines like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity. As AI transforms how people search, GEO helps your brand stay visible in AI-generated answers and discovery pathways.

How do I know if GrowthForge is the right partner for my business?

GrowthForge begins engagements with an Executive Growth Audit. This allows us to analyze your current growth system, identify opportunities, and provide strategic recommendations before discussing a long-term partnership.

What should I expect from a Growth Audit?

A Growth Audit ($750) is a comprehensive strategic assessment that includes business analysis, market analysis, technical SEO audit, AI readiness assessment, paid media review, conversion opportunity analysis, and a 90-day growth roadmap prioritized by impact.

How is the GrowthForge Executive Growth Audit different from using ChatGPT, Claude, or other AI tools?

AI tools can provide general insights, but the GrowthForge Executive Growth Audit combines AI-powered analysis with strategic business evaluation. We analyze your market, competitors, website, acquisition channels, conversion opportunities, and growth constraints to identify priorities and create a focused 90-day growth roadmap aligned with your business goals.

Is it safe to share my analytics and marketing data with GrowthForge?

Your business data is treated as a critical asset. GrowthForge uses information from platforms such as Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, Meta Ads, and other growth systems only to analyze performance, identify revenue opportunities, and develop strategic recommendations. We do not sell, share, or use your business data for purposes unrelated to delivering your Executive Growth Audit.

AI Visibility & Content Use

We want our website to remain discoverable in search while setting clear expectations for AI crawler access and content use.

Can search engines index this website?

Yes. GrowthForge allows standard search engine indexing so our pages can appear in Google Search, Bing, and other search engines, helping businesses discover our AI-powered growth consulting services.

Is AI training allowed on this content?

Yes. GrowthForge expresses ai-train: yes and allows AI-related crawlers in robots.txt.

Which AI crawlers are disallowed?

GrowthForge currently allows major AI and search crawlers, including GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, Google-Extended, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Meta-ExternalAgent, Applebot, Applebot-Extended, Amazonbot, and other standard search engine crawlers.

Will this policy change over time?

Possibly. GrowthForge regularly reviews its AI visibility and indexing policy to ensure our content remains accessible while supporting our long-term business, search, and AI discoverability objectives.
